# Realmspire

Chess across three stacked realms: the Skies, the Realm and the Depths.

**Play it here:** https://rabnerjon.github.io/Games/realmspire/

## Features

- Three 8×8 boards stacked on top of each other, with pieces that move, capture and travel between them
- 12 original piece types, including the Stormcaller's lightning strike and the Warden's freeze
- Two players at one screen, or one player against a bot (Easy, Medium or Hard)
- Legal-move highlighting, undo, board flipping, a move log and a built-in rules guide

## Running it locally

Open `index.html` in a web browser. Everything is in that one file.
